Privacy Policy — No Spoiler for GTA6

Last updated: November 12, 2026

No Spoiler for GTA6 ("the extension") is a browser extension that hides GTA 6 related content on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Web to help users avoid spoilers.

Data collection

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

  • No analytics, tracking or telemetry.
  • No account, login or remote server.
  • No browsing history is recorded.

Data stored locally

The extension stores only the following, using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage):

DataWherePurpose
Your settings (on/off, per-platform toggles, mode, allowed trailers, custom keywords and channel lists) chrome.storage.sync Remember your preferences. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Google syncs them between your own devices.
Number of spoilers hidden (a single counter) chrome.storage.local Show the counter in the popup.

This data never leaves your browser except through Chrome Sync, which is controlled by your Google account settings. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

Page content

To decide what to hide, the extension reads titles, posts, and channel/author names only on supported platforms (youtube.com, twitch.tv, tiktok.com, x.com, twitter.com, facebook.com, instagram.com, web.whatsapp.com), locally in your browser. This text is not stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • storage: save your settings and the counter.
  • Access to supported platforms: read post/video titles locally on those pages to hide or blur GTA 6 related content.
